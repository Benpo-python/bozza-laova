TODIKY Festival — 2023

The first TODIKY Festival, held in Maroantsetra on 18 and 19 February 2023: two days devoted to the district’s living heritage, with carnival, traditional games, exhibitions and artistic expression.

Sheet

Dates
18 and 19 February 2023 (two days)
Place
Maroantsetra
Status
Completed
Attendance
Estimated at more than 2,000 people from the district, according to information provided by LAOVA; a documented count is still to be provided
Updated
October 2026

01The festival

Two days around living heritage.

The activities below are those described by LAOVA and visible in the photographic archive; they are not listed in chronological order.

  • OpeningThe festival opened with public speeches in front of the participants.
  • CarnivalParades in costume, group processions and participants on tall handmade stilts.
  • Traditional gamesOutdoor group games, open to people from across the district.
  • HeritageCultural and craft exhibitions, a display of culinary preparations and of old objects, including coins and utensils.
  • ArtsArtistic performances, a music group on stage, a Vakodrazagna evening, a Maroa Talenta session and a concert.
