01LAOVA at a glance

A Malagasy non-profit association and platform, founded on 2 July 2022 in Maroantsetra by ambitious young people, LAOVA works to safeguard and pass on tangible and intangible, cultural and natural heritage.

  • RootsAntongil Bay, north-east Madagascar
  • MandateHeritage · culture · nature · transmission

What drives us

A heritage has a future only if it stays alive. It takes on its full meaning in shared knowledge, preserved lands and the communities who keep it alive.

Discover our vision

02Why LAOVA

Protecting heritage means keeping knowledge, practices, resources and relationships alive.

What we see

Cultural and natural heritage cannot be separated.

Heritage is not limited to objects: it lives in stories, languages, craft skills, farming practices and environmental knowledge. A single plant can be an ecological resource, a source of income, a building material and the carrier of knowledge passed down through generations.

Its loss is therefore never purely natural or purely cultural. Safeguarding is not just recording endangered practices: it means preserving the conditions for their continuity, recognising those who hold the knowledge and making room for young people.

Our response

Connecting knowledge, people and concrete action.

LAOVA works as a platform: for each mission it brings together the right skills, from research to farming, from crafts to education. Communities take part in defining the initiatives that concern them; their voices are not incidental.

Our approach links research, the environment, education and community development, so that protecting heritage becomes a space for dialogue, participation and action.

03Our six areas of action

One mission, complementary expertise.

Documenting a memory, restoring a natural resource, supporting a productive initiative or passing on a craft do not call for the same skills. Each project brings together the people and partnerships suited to its reality.

A

Heritage, memory and research

Oral memory, cultural practices, architecture and crafts, documented with the consent of knowledge holders.

 B

Nature, biodiversity and living resources

Linking ecosystems, practices and livelihoods; protecting and restoring plant resources.

 C

Education and knowledge transmission

Intergenerational encounters, field-based learning, farming and craft practices.

 D

Community development and responsible economy

Strengthening the productive capacity and autonomy of communities while preserving resources.

 E

Digital documentation, creation and communication

Archives, stories, photographs and video, shared only when knowledge holders agree.

 F

Intercultural encounter

Hasina Immersion: cultural and community mediation, not a holiday offer.

05How LAOVA works

A simple method, from dialogue to transmission.

Our work follows the principles set out in our statutes and institutional documents.

  1. 01

    Listen and decide together

    Communities take part in defining the initiatives that concern them. Each approach starts from expressed needs and real capacities.

  2. 02

    Bring in the right skills

    Depending on the mission: resource persons, researchers, technicians, craftspeople, farmers, educators and partners.

  3. 03

    Document with consent

    Research, testimony, photography and digital archives, produced and shared with the agreement of knowledge holders.

  4. 04

    Act and pass on

    Restoring resources, productive initiatives, learning between generations and intercultural encounters.

  5. 05

    Report honestly

    Objectives and results are presented separately; nothing is announced as achieved on the basis of a plan or budget alone.

07Our territory

Rooted locally, national in scope, open to the world.

LAOVA is based in Maroantsetra, at the head of Antongil Bay. Here, the links between biodiversity, plant resources, housing, crafts, farming, oral heritage and communities can be understood first-hand.

Our statutes cover the heritage of Madagascar as a whole; our interventions, however, are always precisely located. Internationally, we build cooperation between responsible partners, never outside supervision.
