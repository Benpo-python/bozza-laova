In preparation

Protecting the plants behind local crafts and housing.

Protecting a natural resource also means preserving craft practices, building materials and economic prospects. This project connects wetlands, know-how and local income.

Project sheet

Challenge
The plant resources of the wetlands are at once ecological, cultural and economic. Their decline weakens skills passed down from generation to generation.
Territory
Antongil Bay — communes of Ankofabe, Sahasindro and Antanambe; districts of Maroantsetra and Mananara Nord, Ambatosoa region
Framework
Project developed under the Global Environment Facility Small Grants Programme (GEF SGP, Operational Phase 8)
Planned activities
Participatory mapping and governance; nurseries and replanting; ecological monitoring; training; support for responsible economic use
Groups involved
Craftspeople, harvesters, wetland communities, community liaisons
Skills
Environmental technicians, ecologists, GIS specialists, monitoring officers, community liaisons, craft resource persons
Status
In preparation
Updated
October 2026

Catalogue of materials

Three plants, three families of gestures.

Names, uses and the state of each resource will be completed with knowledge holders as documentation progresses.

Penja

Lepironia mucronata · Marsh sedge

Habitat
Wetlands and freshwater marshes
Part used
Stems, harvested, dried and flattened
Gestures
Weaving mats, baskets and containers
State of the resource
To be documented with communities
Raphia

Raphia farinifera · Palm

Habitat
Riverbanks and damp ground
Part used
Fibre stripped from young leaves, dried, sometimes dyed
Gestures
Plaiting, weaving, sewing: mats, bags, hats, textiles
State of the resource
To be documented with communities
Ravinala

Ravinala madagascariensis · Endemic Strelitziaceae

Habitat
Secondary forest, forest edges and open land in the east
Part used
Leaves, leaf stalks and trunk
Gestures
Roofing, walls of assembled leaf stalks (falafa), floors
State of the resource
To be documented with communities
