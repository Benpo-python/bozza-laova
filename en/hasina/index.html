Hasina ImmersionAn initiative of Plateforme LAOVA

A territory is more than its landscapes.

It also reveals itself in the stories people tell, the gestures they pass on, the work that sustains families and the initiatives that are building tomorrow.

Hasina Immersion is a gateway to Plateforme LAOVA and to the human, cultural and community realities of Maroantsetra. We facilitate personalised encounters and experiences, shaped by your interests and by what our teams and local hosts can genuinely offer.

This is not a pre-set tourist programme. Every request is built on listening, mutual understanding, respect for people and discovery of LAOVA’s work in the territory.

01An approach in four movements

A way of making sense, not an itinerary.

These movements are not imposed on everyone. They prevent immersion from being reduced to the consumption of “authentic places”.

  • UnderstandTalk with members, resource persons and partners, and discover the heritage, ecological and social issues behind each initiative.
  • Take partJoin some association or community activities, according to their calendar and hosting conditions.
  • ContributeWhen real needs have been identified and a suitable contribution is possible — without replacing local paid work or creating artificial activity.
  • DiscoverExplore aspects of the territory and its culture with respectful guidance, through optional activities suited to your interests.

02Our protocol

What guides every encounter.

What we do

  • The people and communities met can accept, decline or adapt any activity.
  • Consent is asked for every photograph and every testimony; some knowledge and places are not meant to be made public.
  • LAOVA remains your point of reference: welcome, orientation, coordination of contacts, information on local conditions.
  • Real costs — accommodation, food, local transport, coordination, guidance, community contributions — are identified and explained.
  • Particular care applies to minors, personal data and educational activities.

What Hasina is not

  • A travel agency or LAOVA’s “tourism branch”.
  • A catalogue of excursions, packages or prices.
  • The purchase of volunteering or of access to communities.
  • An activity that would displace local paid work or entrust sensitive tasks to unqualified people.

03How it works

Understand, share, talk, build.

Travel to Maroantsetra may remain at the participant’s expense, depending on what is agreed. Guidance, logistics, accommodation and meals are not guaranteed by default: they are agreed case by case.

  1. 01

    Understand

    You get to know LAOVA, its projects and its principles.

  2. 02

    Share your interests

    Heritage, crafts, environment, farming, research, education…

  3. 03

    Talk with LAOVA

    Together we look at what is feasible, relevant and accepted.

  4. 04

    Build a possibility

    An encounter that makes sense, for you as well as for your hosts.
